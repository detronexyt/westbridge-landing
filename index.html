01 Who we are

A trusted senior secured financing partner for experienced real estate sponsors.

Westbridge Finance is a specialist private credit platform providing senior secured financing across a broad range of real estate asset classes and investment stages throughout Poland.

We focus on transactions where traditional lenders cannot provide the speed, flexibility or structure a deal requires. Keeping decision-makers directly involved lets us assess opportunities quickly, structure solutions efficiently and give sponsors certainty of execution.
