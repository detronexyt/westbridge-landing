01 Who we are

A trusted senior secured financing partner for experienced real estate sponsors.

Westbridge Finance is a specialist private credit platform providing senior secured financing across a broad range of real estate asset classes and investment stages throughout Poland.

We focus on opportunities where traditional lenders are unable to provide the speed, flexibility or structure required to support a transaction. By keeping decision-makers directly involved throughout the process, we are able to assess opportunities quickly, structure solutions efficiently and provide certainty of execution.

We work alongside sponsors to structure financing that reflects the specific needs of each transaction, with a streamlined investment approach designed to deliver funding in up to 30 days from the initial approach.

02 Why Westbridge

Where bank lending stops, we start.

Banks remain structurally constrained on land, early-stage and transitional assets. We built a lender for exactly those moments, with institutional underwriting and a short decision path.
