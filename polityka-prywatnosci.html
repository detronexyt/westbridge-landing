What data we process and why

We process the data you give us in the enquiry form and in direct correspondence: your name, company, e-mail address, phone number and the details of the project you describe. We use them to reply to your enquiry, assess whether the transaction fits our mandate and hold talks ahead of a possible financing agreement.

  • Correspondence and establishing or defending claims: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest.

How long we keep the data

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to handle the matter and then until any claims become time-barred. If we enter into an agreement, its documents are kept for the period required by law, including tax and anti-money-laundering rules. Data processed on the basis of consent is kept until you withdraw it.
